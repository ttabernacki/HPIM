How to play

Steer with ←↑→↓ / WASD or swipe. The board wraps around.
Echoes replay your own path, trailing behind you with a gap. Touching one ends the run. A new echo appears every 10 foods.
Eat food. Reach it quickly to build a combo and multiply your score.
Golden food is worth big points and refills a charge, but it spawns in dangerous spots and vanishes.
Space / tap the button to Phase: pass through echoes for 4 moves. Phasing through one scores a bonus. Grazing next to an echo scores too. You earn a charge every 4 foods.
